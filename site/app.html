Client-side only, zero servers

Every check runs in your browser. Stellagate makes direct HTTPS calls to public Horizon servers, Stellar RPC nodes, and public directory endpoints. We run no server of our own. That protects your privacy. Secret keys are detected on paste and wiped from the input immediately. They never reach the network or the browser address bar.
