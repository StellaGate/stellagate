This page has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It describes what the site actually does, as plainly as we can.

No cookies

Stellagate sets no cookies, and none of the services it calls receive cookies from your browser, because the page requests them without credentials.

Local storage

The site keeps one item in your browser's local storage:

  • stellagate-notice-seen, set to 1 when you press OK on the privacy notice, so it does not appear on every visit. It never leaves your device and has no expiry. Clear your browser's site data to remove it.

That is the only thing stored, and it is there because you asked to dismiss the notice. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking storage of any kind, so there is nothing to consent to beyond that.

Fonts

The site's fonts are served from the site itself. Your browser does not contact a font service.
