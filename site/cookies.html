Consent storage

Stellagate uses zero tracking or analytics cookies. When you accept the privacy notice, the site stores your acceptance so you are never asked again:

  • stellagate_cookie_consent and stellagate-notice-seen in local storage.
  • A persistent, first-party cookie stellagate_cookie_consent=1 (10-year lifetime, SameSite=Lax) so the notice stays dismissed even if local storage is cleared.

That is the only thing stored, strictly to remember your dismissal. There is no analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, or tracking storage of any kind.

Fonts

The site's fonts are served from the site itself. Your browser does not contact a font service.
