Three payments that go wrong every day, and the line Stellagate shows before they do.

The exchange needed a memo

Exchanges keep every customer's deposits in one account and tell them apart by the memo. Leave it off and the money arrives, but nobody can tell it is yours.

The wallet could not hold the asset

A Stellar account has to opt in to each asset before it can receive it, and some issuers have to approve it as well. Until then, the payment fails.

A smart wallet cannot add a memo

Passkey wallets pay through Soroban contracts, and those payments have no memo field. Stellagate tells you to send from a regular account, or to use the exchange's M-address if it has one.

How it works

One paste, five questions. Every check runs in your browser against the live network.

  1. 01
    GDT7 ARDY ZRBX XYOC ...

    Paste the address

    G, M, C or name*domain. Paste a secret key by mistake and Stellagate clears it before anything goes over the network.

  2. 02
    FineDoes it exist?Yes, it is live on the network.

    Does it exist?

    An account that has not been created yet cannot receive an asset, and a plain XLM payment to it fails too.

  3. 03
    StopDoes it need a memo?Yes, and no memo was given.

    Does it need a memo?

    Stellagate reads the account's own SEP-29 flag and the public directory of more than a hundred exchange accounts.

  4. 04
    StopCan it hold the asset?Not this much. It would pass the account's own limit.

    Can it hold the asset?

    It checks the trustline, the issuer's approval and the limit. For a contract address, it checks the asset's Stellar Asset Contract instead.

  5. 05
    CheckCan your wallet send to it?Probably, but many exchanges do not credit these yet.

    Can your wallet send to it?

    Smart wallets cannot attach a memo. If yours is one, Stellagate says so and gives you the route that works.

  6. 06
    StopIs it on a warning list?Yes. Do not send.

    Is it on a warning list?

    Accounts the public directory flags as malicious or unsafe are stopped outright, whatever else checks out.

It reads the network. It never signs.

Runs in your browser

The checks call Horizon, Stellar RPC and the StellarExpert directory straight from your browser. Stellagate has no server of its own, so there is nothing of ours to log what you paste.

Secret keys are refused

Paste a secret key (it starts with S) and the check stops, clears the field and keeps the key out of the address bar. No request is made with it.

No custody, no signing

Stellagate never asks for a signature, holds no keys and cannot send a transaction. Your wallet does the sending.

Tested against the real network

We ran each check on testnet, then sent the payment anyway. Where Stellagate said Stop over a trustline or a missing account, the network refused the payment too. Two kinds of Stop cover payments the network accepts: a deposit without its memo, and tokens sent to a contract address with nothing deployed. We sent the second. The network took it, and the tokens are stuck.

For wallet and dapp developers

Call it before you build the transaction. You get a status, a reason code and a fix written for your user.

import { check } from "@stellagate/core";

const verdict = await check({
  to: "GDT7ARDYZRBXXYOCSQ3MUMISTITSSRWZI6KR2A5L5Q3KB4QIZHGYMTIH",
  from: userAddress,
  asset: "native",
  network: "public",
});

if (verdict.status === "block") {
  showStop(verdict.reasons[0].message, verdict.reasons[0].fix);
}

Not on npm yet. The library and its tests are in the repository.

block
Stop the send. It would fail on chain, or reach a pooled account that cannot credit it.
warn
Ask the user to confirm. The account may need funding first, or a smart wallet is paying an M-address that some exchanges do not credit yet.
ok
Nothing Stellagate checked would stop this payment.

Questions

Does Stellagate hold or send funds?

No. It reads public account data and gives a verdict. It never signs, submits or holds anything.

Why is the SEP-29 flag not enough?

Only 14 of the 107 exchange accounts known to need a memo set it. Stellagate also reads the public directory, which catches the other 93.

Can a smart wallet pay an exchange?

Only if the exchange gives you an M-address, or does not need a memo. Smart wallets pay through Soroban, and Soroban transactions cannot carry a memo. Many exchanges do not yet credit M-address transfers from smart wallets, so ask the exchange before you send a large amount.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence, with no fees and no token.
