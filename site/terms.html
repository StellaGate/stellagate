What Stellagate is

A free page that looks up public Stellar data about an address and tells you about problems it finds before you send a payment. It never asks for your secret key, never signs or sends anything, and never holds funds.

It is a check, not a guarantee

The result is only as good as the public data behind it. An exchange may need a memo without being listed anywhere. An issuer can change its rules after you check. A service the page relies on may be down or wrong. "Looks fine" means nothing we checked found a problem. It does not promise the payment will be credited.

Always use the address and memo the receiver gives you, and for a large amount, send a small test payment first.
